# Tax Monkey beta

Tax Monkey is a Mac app for preparing your federal and Virginia income tax
returns. Everything stays on your Mac: your return is saved locally, and
nothing is sent anywhere.

## Download

Get the latest `.dmg` from **[Releases](../../releases/latest)**.

1. Open the DMG and drag **Tax Monkey** to **Applications**.
2. Open Tax Monkey from Applications.

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later). The app is signed and
notarized by Apple.

## Before you use it

- **This is a beta.** Check every number against your own records before
  you file. Tax Monkey doesn't replace a tax professional.
- **Federal and Virginia only.** Other state returns aren't supported.
- **Filing is by mail.** Tax Monkey creates the forms for you to print, sign
  and mail. E-filing isn't available yet.
- **No automatic updates.** Come back to Releases for new versions. Your
  saved returns are kept when you install a new version over the old one.
